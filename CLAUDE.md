Branding: the header lockup and home hero follow the Hypnos Medical brand kit (source of truth: kieran1010/nexus360-app docs/brand.md), on-dark variant; the app keeps its own dark theme. The moon is only the kit's path `M12.2 0A12 12 0 1 0 23.96 13.29A9.14 9.14 0 0 1 12.2 0Z`. "Hypnos" and the hero use Gelasio 400, "MEDICAL" and "Vantage" Geist 600, embedded in index.html as base64 subsets containing only those words' glyphs, so changing that text means re-subsetting the fonts. Don't hand-change the lockup's sizes or colours; change the kit. Favicons must stay real files (not data: URIs) so Google Search can show them.

Installable app (PWA): `manifest.webmanifest` + `sw.js`. index.html and calc/calculators.js are network-first and cached only as a pair; images and icons are precached at install. A new image must be added to `IMAGES` in sw.js (`calc/pwa.test.js` fails otherwise); replacing an image or icon under the same filename needs a `CACHE_VERSION` bump to reach installed copies. App icons are rendered from favicon.svg.
