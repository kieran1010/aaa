Brand kit: the Hypnos Medical brand kit (spec, master artwork, product app icons) lives in kieran1010/hypnos `brand-kit/` (spec: `brand-kit/README.md`), the source of truth for every Hypnos Medical product. Change the brand there first, then copy into this repo.

Branding: the header lockup and home hero follow the Hypnos Medical brand kit (see Brand kit above), on-dark variant; the app keeps its own dark theme. The flat moon is only the kit's path `M12.2 0A12 12 0 1 0 23.96 13.29A9.14 9.14 0 0 1 12.2 0Z`. The home hero is the kit's "hero display": "Vantage" alone in Gelasio at clamp(48px, 8vw, 80px), not a logo (no moon beside it, no link), with the photo moon as a 240px watermark at 18% top right. The kit's hero watermark has a navy ground that would show as a box on this app's background, so `images/moon-photo-watermark.png` is the kit's transparent cut-out master downscaled to 480px (owner's choice). "Hypnos" and the hero use Gelasio 400, "MEDICAL" and "Vantage" Geist 600, embedded in index.html as base64 subsets containing only those words' glyphs, so changing that text means re-subsetting the fonts. Don't hand-change the lockup's sizes or colours; change the kit. Favicons must stay real files (not data: URIs) so Google Search can show them.

Installable app (PWA): `manifest.webmanifest` + `sw.js`. index.html and calc/calculators.js are network-first and cached only as a pair; images and icons are precached at install. A new image must be added to `IMAGES` in sw.js (`calc/pwa.test.js` fails otherwise); replacing an image or icon under the same filename needs a `CACHE_VERSION` bump to reach installed copies. The home-screen icons (`icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`) are Vantage's own product icon from the kit (kieran1010/hypnos `brand-kit/product-icons/vantage-*.png`: syringe over a faint moon on #0c1220), copied unchanged; change them there. The favicons stay the plain moon on navy.
